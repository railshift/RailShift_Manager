Who We Are
▾

Shift Management is a mobile application designed for railway crew members to manage duty schedules, shift assignments, and operational alerts. It is an internal workforce management tool.

App: Shift Management  ·  Package: com.shiftmanagement.app  ·  Platform: Android

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

3
How We Use Your Information
▾

Your data is used exclusively to provide the Shift Management service:

  • Authenticate and manage your account securely
  • Display your assigned duty schedules and shift information
  • Send local notifications for upcoming duties and alerts
  • Allow administrators to manage and assign crew duties
  • Provide password reset functionality via email OTP
  • Maintain your login session securely for up to 1 day
We never use your data for: advertising, profiling, marketing, or any purpose unrelated to crew shift management.
4
Data Storage & Security
▾

We implement industry-standard security practices to protect your information:

  • Auth tokens are stored in your device's secure local storage (SharedPreferences)
  • All app–server communication is protected by HTTPS / TLS encryption
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text — server-side hashing is used
  • JWT tokens have automatic expiry to limit exposure if compromised
  • Our backend is hosted on Render, a trusted cloud infrastructure provider

Sessions automatically expire after 1 day of inactivity, requiring re-authentication for your security.

5
Notifications & Background Activity
▾
  • Local Notifications: Generated entirely on-device to remind you of upcoming duties. No external push notification servers are involved.
  • Background Polling: Android WorkManager polls our server every 15 minutes (when connected to the internet) to fetch new alerts. This uses WAKE_LOCK and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED permissions.
  • Notification Permission: On Android 13+, we request POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission before sending any notifications.

You can disable all notifications at any time via your device's Settings app.

6
Data Sharing
▾
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information — period.

Your data is only shared in these limited circumstances:

  • Within your organisation: Authorised administrators can view crew duty assignments as part of operations.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if required by law or valid legal process.
  • Infrastructure provider: Render (our cloud host) processes data on our behalf under strict confidentiality obligations.
7
Your Rights
▾
  • Access: View your profile and account data within the app at any time.
  • Correction: Update your profile details through the app's profile screen.
  • Deletion: Request full account and data deletion by contacting us via email.
  • Logout: Logging out immediately clears all local authentication tokens from your device.
8
Children's Privacy
▾

Shift Management is intended exclusively for adult railway crew members and operational staff (18+). The app is not directed at children.

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.
